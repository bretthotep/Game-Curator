## Usage notes

- The visuals intentionally avoid reproducing game covers, logos, recognizable characters or existing product interfaces.
- Treat the colour and typography choices as hypotheses. Validate contrast, keyboard flow, zoom/reflow, reduced motion and screen-reader behavior before implementation.
- Replace fictional title examples with real catalogue data only when that data and associated imagery have appropriate provenance and usage permission.
- For external references, record the source URL, creator, date accessed, specific lesson, permission/rights status, and whether the repo may redistribute the actual image. If permission is uncertain, keep a link and written notes rather than copying the asset.
- Promotion from concept to approved design requires an explicit product-owner decision recorded in the design documentation.
